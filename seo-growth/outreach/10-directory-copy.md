**Name:** Shopify Product Downloader
**Tagline (60):** Export any Shopify store's products to CSV, free
**Short (160):** Free Chrome extension that exports any public Shopify store's products to an import-ready CSV or JSON, and analyses its theme, apps and SEO.
**Long (≈600):**
Shopify Product Downloader is a free Chrome extension for dropshippers, merchants and agencies doing competitor and supplier research. Add any public Shopify store and export all products or a single collection, including variants, SKUs, prices, vendors and image URLs, as a CSV in Shopify's import format or as JSON. Sort by the store's own best-selling order to see what sells first. The Store Analyser shows the theme, the detected apps, and SEO, product and content scores. It works only with data stores publish publicly. It doesn't show inventory or sales.
