## What the winners do that we don't

1. **They own a website that ranks.** Each competitor above 5k users links to a domain from its listing. Koala and Meetanshi publish "best X" listicles on their own blogs that rank their own tool. That is the main backlink and traffic engine in this niche, and it's how a 480-user extension (Meetanshi) still ranks for "best shopify scraper extensions".
2. **No-friction promise.** The 6k-user free scraper leads with "no login, no API, no coding". We put a Google sign-in wall in front of the first download.
3. **Visible changelog.** Shopify Spy lists quarterly changes in its description. That signals active maintenance to users and to Google.
4. **Ratings volume.** Koala has 770 ratings. Ratings drive ranking inside the Chrome Web Store.

## Backlink gap (discoverable without paid tools)

The editorial pages that rank for our core terms and list competitors but not us:

- dodropshipping.com, *best Chrome extensions for dropshipping* and *best Shopify spy tools*. Editorial, independent. **Best target.**
- meetanshi.com, *5 Best Shopify Scraper Chrome Extensions*. Competitor-owned, so low odds.
- thunderbit.com, two Shopify-scraper roundups. Competitor-owned (generic scraper vendor).
- blog.pixiebrix.com, *best Shopify Chrome extensions for store owners 2026*.
- actually-useful-extensions.pages.dev, the Shopify scraping tutorial.

A full referring-domain export needs a paid backlink index (Ahrefs, Semrush or Moz). I haven't bought one. If you want this, a free Ahrefs Webmaster Tools account only covers sites you own, so it becomes useful once our site exists.

## Positioning we can win honestly

Competitors split into two camps: "exporters" (CSV only) and "spy suites" (revenue and ads, often paid). Ours does both export **and** a store analyser (theme, apps, SEO score) for free. After the compliance fix, the defensible message is:

> **Free Shopify catalog export + store analyser in one extension, with no paywall on export size.**

The exporters can't claim the analyser and the spy suites can't claim free export, so this angle avoids their head terms.
