How it works

  1. Open the extension and add a store URL.
  2. Pick a collection, All Products or Best Selling.
  3. Choose CSV or JSON and click Download.

What it can't do

It only reads data a store publishes publicly. There's no inventory, cost or sales data, and some stores block their public feed. Product photos and descriptions usually belong to the brand, so use exports for research, not to clone listings.
